HISTORICAL BCPL MATERIAL
The original BCPL transport-tape material is associated with Martin
Richards. Ken Yap archived the tape. Robert Nordier corrected character
mappings, organized the files, and publicly preserved them as bcpltape.
The original archival README and tape INDEX are retained in GitHub.
Historical source files remain subject to their original copyrights.
No blanket unrestricted license for all the old tape files was found.
Public archival access is evidence of provenance, not relicensing.

DERIVED CAMBRIDGE SOURCE
Demoted SYN, LEX, TRNA, TRNB, BCPL, BLIB and CGA through CGE are generated
by the project's make-demoted.py from the historical Cambridge sources.
These are adaptations for the interpreter-hosted bootstrap compiler.
Reconstruction does not remove historical design/source provenance.
The generated source is distinct from the unmodified archival source.

COMPILER STATUS
The demoted Cambridge compiler executes as INTCODE under ICINT19 and
produces System/370 assembler. That is not a native-running compiler.
An independently native-running Cambridge compiler is still unfinished.
Historical MAKEOBJ and MAKELIB are reference texts, not ready-to-run
MVS installation jobs. CAMBBLD is development JCL requiring adaptation.
