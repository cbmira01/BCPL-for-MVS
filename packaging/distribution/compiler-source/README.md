The [RC4 manifest](../manifest/releases/BCPL-0.1.0-rc4.yaml), [original transport tape](../../../richards-bcpltape/) and [source notice](SNOTICE.txt) establish selection and provenance. Historical redistribution rights remain unresolved; see the [RC4 companion](../publication/RC4-README.md) and [third-party notices](../../../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

The RC4 producer imports earlier RC2/RC3 helpers, including the BT0021 stage runner transitively. Do not rerun population or staging or delete those helpers without dependency review. The observed EBCDIC and transport decisions and qualifications are retained in the [reconciliation record](rc2-job9330-reconciliation-acceptance-and-encoding-decision-20261120.md) and [BT0021 acceptance](../create-tape/ta2-bt0021-qualified-acceptance-20261121.md).
