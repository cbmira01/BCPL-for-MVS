## Purpose and binding acceptance criteria

Verify that the **tape-installed native component inventory**
is independently usable under actual **MVS 3.8J**, not just
present in the recipient PDS directories:

1. **Core Runtime:** establish accepted release-object provenance,
   link against the distributed `BCPLMAIN` and necessary
   `BCPLMEM`, `BCPLBYTE`, `BCPLAPT`, `BCPLIO`,
   `BCPLRGS` and `BCPLREG` members; demonstrate
   correct native application execution.
2. **Base Library:** verify historical `BLIB` as a distinct
   BCPL-derived base library, and document its limitations for
   source rebuild when the native compiler is absent.
3. **Coroutine Support Library:** verify separately distributed
   `BCPLCO` and `COROUTIN`, source-to-generated-assembler
   provenance, accepted coroutine behavior, and native linkage.
4. **System Extensions:** verify distributed optional
   `BCPLDEMO`, `BCPLTIME` and `BCPLQSAM` from
   `EXT_OBJ` and supporting source/JCL; demonstrate
   selected versus unselected packages under accepted
   **BCEX/BCRG v1**, with real IEWL and MVS execution.
5. **Distribution reproducibility:** reconcile member bytes,
   documented build JCL, provenance and accepted native
   test oracles. Prove supported rebuilds through real
   IFOX/IEWL/MVS without pretending to provide a runnable
   native Cambridge Compiler. Record conditional limitations
   honestly, including `BLIB` and generated `COROUTIN`.
