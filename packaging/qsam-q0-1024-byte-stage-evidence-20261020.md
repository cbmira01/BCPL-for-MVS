# Milestone QSAM — Q.0 1024-byte stage-boundary execution evidence

**March:** Q.0 — Reconnaissance & contract (open).
**MVS execution:** 20 October 2026, MVS 3.8J/TK5.
**Design:** Owner approved a **1024-byte total private logical-record stage
per handle** after these experiments. This is an initial BCPLQSAM
implementation design limit, **not** the maximum allowed by MVS or
evidence of a completed production extension.

## What the V/VB tests establish

Each V/VB writer created 15 logical records on an isolated temporary
`&&Q0CEIL` DASD data set; a separately assembled and linked reader
checked the actual post-OPEN DCB RECFM, LRECL, BLKSIZE, RDW lengths,
reserved RDW bytes, record order, and EODAD. The length sequence
repeated five times: **4, 16, and 1024 bytes including RDW**.

For each maximum-size record, the reader checked the full **1020-byte
application data area**, comprising the known fill pattern and
specific binary `X'00'` and `X'FF'` boundary sentinels. QSAM
round trips included valid RDW-only records with **zero data bytes**.
No RDW or BDW was exposed through a BCPL API.

These results support a stage of 1024 total bytes: V/VB data capacity
is at most **1020** because the stage must include the four-byte RDW.
They do not prove every binary byte pattern or every MVS device/format.

## What JOB 2182 establishes

A standalone IFOX routine simultaneously allocated sixteen independent
1024-byte areas with checked `GETMAIN RC`, verified endpoint
sentinels with all areas still live, then called `FREEMAIN R` exactly
once per area. It returned GO RC=0000 and the expected QOUT marker.
The **16,384 stage bytes** exclude DCB/control regions, QSAM internal
buffers, and the BCPL runtime and application.

This does **not** show sixteen simultaneously OPEN datasets, a
production per-handle allocation/lifetime implementation, or that
sixteen complete handles fit under `REGION=256K`.

## Generation issue (not an MVS error)

The generator's local `stage16 --check` succeeded, but normal
generation initially stopped with `ValueError: unexpected JOB card`
because `QS0S16` is padded to eight JCL name columns by
`authenticated_job_card`. JOB 2182 was generated with a one-time,
ignored-`workarea` bypass without altering tracked files. The
generator is corrected separately to validate the full eight-column
job-name field and to use the same padding in `--check`.

## Accepted contract and remaining limits

The owner accepted **1024 bytes of total private stage per handle**
after reviewing these results. Consequently:

- F/FB: effective LRECL up to 1024; application data length equals
  effective LRECL.
- V/VB: effective LRECL up to 1024 **including four RDW bytes**;
  application data length can range from 0 to LRECL minus 4.
- Effective LRECL greater than 1024 is outside the initial BCPLQSAM
  supported contract and must be rejected without issuing a GET/PUT.
- The public `INFO.result!7` reports the **dataset-specific maximum
  application data bytes** for a supported open handle, not the
  stage size nor a machine-wide maximum.
- Exact full-job region budget, sixteen real concurrent handles,
  extraordinary CLOSE/abend recovery, and shared Runtime Extensions
  registration/finalization remain open.

**No production BCPLQSAM implementation, BOOT_OBJ change, Q.0 closure
or Q.1 work is claimed.**
